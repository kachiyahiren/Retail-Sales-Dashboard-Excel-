# 🛍️ Retail Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Excel-based Retail Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions such as time, gender, product category, and transaction behavior.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance using KPIs, charts, and dynamic filters to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Monitor key business metrics (sales, transactions, quantity)
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Compare performance across gender and product categories
- Evaluate quarterly product performance
- Enable interactive data exploration

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📌 1. Average Sales: Gender vs Month
- Monthly trend comparison between male and female customers

👉 Insight:
- Sales fluctuate across months
- Female sales show stronger peaks in certain months

---

### 📌 2. Sales by Product Category (Gender-wise)
- Categories:
  - Beauty
  - Clothing
  - Electronics

👉 Insight:
- Clothing category performs best overall
- Female customers dominate in Beauty & Clothing
- Male contribution is stronger in Electronics

---

### 📌 3. Quantity Distribution by Category
- Clothing leads in quantity sold
- Electronics and Beauty follow closely

---

### 📌 4. Quarterly Product Performance
- Comparison of category performance across Q1–Q4

👉 Insight:
- Clothing shows consistent performance across quarters
- Electronics peaks in specific quarters
